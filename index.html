Overtime Call List

Overtime goes to the person with the fewest charged hours first. Accepting and refusing both add charged hours, so whoever has taken the most overtime sits at the bottom of the list. Ties go to seniority.

Timesheets

Base time is 40 h a week. Every hour past 40 in the week is paid at 1.5×, in the order the hours were worked, whatever the entry type. A call-list job worked in a short week can therefore be paid at straight time, while it still counts on the call list. Daily rules are off by default and can be set under Rules.
